Ash Help Portal

A superhero-themed support portal built around empathy and asking for help.

Ash helps people express what they are going through, connect with an empathetic virtual assistant, and submit a help request when they need urgent assistance.

You deserve to be heard
🤍

Why Ash Exists

Reaching out can feel difficult. Ash offers a welcoming place to put struggles into words and find a next step toward support.

★

Ways to Get Support

  • Express what you are going through.
  • Talk with an empathetic virtual assistant.
  • Submit a request for urgent assistance.
⚙️

Under the Hood

  • Flask-powered Python web application.
  • SQLite stores submitted help requests.
  • Email notifications use SMTP or a FormSubmit fallback.
⚠️

Urgent Situations

Urgent requests are saved and sent to the support team by email. If someone is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services as well.
